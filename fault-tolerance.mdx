A zone is built to keep serving the workloads it already knows about, even when it
cannot reach anything outside the data center, has no free GPUs left, or loses a
machine. This page states precisely what continues and what does not in each of
those cases, so you can plan around them rather than discover them.

## What the WAN link is used for

The [Thunder Registry](/architecture) is the only Thunder component outside your data
center. A zone contacts it over `443` to register servers and clients, to check
credentials against the list of clients that are still active, and to report
telemetry.

GPU selection is not one of those things. Servers in a zone share the state of their
GPUs over the zone mesh and choose a GPU locally, and CUDA traffic goes directly
between the client and the server holding the GPU. Neither path leaves the zone.

## WAN outage

If a zone loses its WAN link, or otherwise cannot reach
`registry.thundercompute.com`:

### Not affected

- **Running workloads.** Every CUDA process keeps running against its GPU. Nothing is
  interrupted, paused, or slowed.
- **Handing out GPUs.** Clients that are already registered keep acquiring and
  releasing GPUs. Selection happens in the zone mesh, and clients are authorized
  against a roster each server keeps on disk, so even a server restart mid-outage
  does not lock them out.
- **Finding servers.** Clients use their cached view of the zone rather than asking
  the Registry.
- **Reconnecting.** A client that still holds a valid assignment reconnects straight
  to the same server.

### Affected

- **Registering new servers.** A new GPU server cannot join a zone. Enrollment
  requires the Registry.
- **Registering new clients.** Enrollment tokens are issued and redeemed through the
  Registry, so no new client can be brought online.
- **Revoking a server or client.** The revocation is accepted but not applied in the
  zone until the link returns.
- **Dashboard and telemetry.** The dashboard shows the zone as last seen, and
  telemetry gathers no new data for the duration.

A zone refreshes its client roster about once a minute, and retries every few seconds
while the Registry is unreachable, so it picks up revocations and newly registered
clients shortly after the link is restored.

<Warning>
  Because the cached roster is authoritative during an outage, a client revoked while
  the link is down keeps its access until the zone can reach the Registry again. Treat
  revocation as eventually consistent, not instantaneous.
</Warning>

One case does not survive an outage: a zone that has never obtained a roster, from
the Registry or from an on-disk cache, cannot authorize anyone. That only applies to
a brand new zone brought up while the Registry is already unreachable.

## No free GPUs

Oversubscription means a zone can be fully committed at a given moment. When a client
asks for a GPU and none is free, the request is not failed. The client waits and
retries every 10 seconds until capacity appears, then continues.

From the workload's point of view, the CUDA call that triggered the connection
blocks. Nothing is returned to the application as an error, so a job does not crash
because the fleet was busy.

The client stays quiet for the first 10 seconds of waiting, then reports:

```text
All GPUs are currently busy. Retrying...
```

After a minute it also prints when the wait started and how to shorten it. In
non-interactive environments, such as a scheduler or CI log, it prints one status
line per update instead of redrawing.

Press `Ctrl-C` to stop waiting and exit. To reduce waiting overall, lower the
oversubscription ratio in the zone, or request a GPU type with more free capacity.

## Losing a machine

| Event | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Client crashes or is killed | The server stops receiving heartbeats for that session and tears it down on its own, returning the GPU to the pool within about two minutes. |
| Server becomes unreachable mid-session | The workload's CUDA calls fail. Restart the workload; it is assigned to another server with free capacity. |
| Server is drained or removed | Existing sessions on it end. New assignments go to the remaining servers in the zone. |

Thunder does not migrate a live CUDA session between servers. Checkpoint and restore
of GPU state is not supported; see [Compatibility](/compatibility).
