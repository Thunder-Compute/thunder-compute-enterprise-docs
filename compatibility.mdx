## Compatibility

- Any NVIDIA GPU and most data centers.
- Recommended round-trip latency below 2ms.
- Recommended throughput greater than 5Gbps.
- No workload changes required.
- Compatible with most CUDA applications.
- Validated by 250,000+ GPU hours of customer workloads.
- Common workloads are reliable and well-tested.
- Untested workloads may occasionally be incompatible.
- Observability supports diagnosis and regular fixes.

## Performance

- Application-dependent overhead, typically 0–20%.
- Includes LLM inference and fine-tuning workloads.
- Qwen3.5 27B text generation: approximately 10% tokens-per-second overhead.
- Qwen3.5 4B fine-tuning: approximately 7% step-time overhead.
- Performance complaints are rare in our cloud.
