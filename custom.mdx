## Thunder Compute SDK

Use the [Thunder Compute SDK](https://github.com/Thunder-Compute/thunder-sdk)
to integrate GPU virtualization into custom environments.

The SDK gives your control plane the primitives needed to manage nodes, clients, and zones. 
Review the [requirements](/requirements),[server setup](/servers), [client setup](/clients),
and [authentication claims](/authentication#claims) before designing the integration.

## Support

Thunder Compute actively supports custom deployment work. We can help design the
integration, adapt it to your scheduler and infrastructure, and work directly in
customer codebases when deeper changes are needed.
